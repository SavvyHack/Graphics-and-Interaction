# Level 3 — Escape Circuit

Status: proposed blockout; target 6–9 minutes. Recombines learned routing, one latch and two independent block-held gates. Introduce no new mandatory power-up.

## Rooms and intended solo solution
1. Service loop: crate A on lower plate disables a short access laser. Pass it to reach pair A; transfer to upper maintenance route and latch a bridge open. Return through A and cross that bridge to checkpoint 1. Crate A stays on its own bounded track.
2. Relay room: checkpoint 1 starts an independent room with pair B reachable from the entry. Push crate B onto the final-laser plate, then use B to the route before that laser. Passing the laser reaches checkpoint 2 on a safe landing. No single crate is needed in two distant positions.
3. Escape run: one predictable timed electric gate and a short sequence of already-taught jumps. Supply a safe waiting area and clear warning before activation. Exit triggers an escape beat and campaign-complete menu, not another stage load.

## Reset boundaries and difficulty
Checkpoint 1 baseline preserves the bridge from room 1 and resets room 2 only. Checkpoint 2 baseline preserves solved room 2 access and resets the final traversal segment only. Neither portal emerges in a timed hazard. Keep timing forgiving; difficulty comes from seeing the route sequence, not hidden switches or precise inputs. Level retry resets all rooms and restores three lives.

## Acceptance
Demonstrate the complete solo solution, fail at each checkpoint, reset with both crates moved, backtrack and try every pair in both directions. No softlock after closing a gate; no softlock after three lives are lost; ending and credits return to Home and Replay. New Game after completion clears campaign progress only. Optional best-time display belongs after all mandatory paths pass.
