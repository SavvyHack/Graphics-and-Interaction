# Level 2 — Relay Chambers

Status: proposed blockout; target 5–7 minutes. Introduces persistent switches plus route ordering, with one forgiving moving-platform crossing.

## Rooms and intended solo solution
1. Lower hub: pair A connects the hub to a maintenance balcony. On the balcony interact with a latched switch; it permanently extends a bridge across the hub gap for this room baseline.
2. Return through A, cross the new bridge to a crate track. Place the crate on its plate to suppress the laser on the upper route. Pair B is now reachable from this side of the hub; it was not locked by a timer.
3. Use B to the upper route, cross the disabled laser and reach a checkpoint on safe ground. Then ride a slow shuttle with visible endpoints to the level exit.

## Solo adaptation
The latch replaces a second character holding a bridge control. The crate replaces a second character holding the laser plate. Two labelled pairs prevent arbitrary free-placement shortcuts; only one new cause/effect relationship is introduced per room. Position pad arrivals so both routes are recoverable.

## Reset boundaries
Before upper checkpoint: reset the latch/bridge and crate together and return the rat to the lower hub, where A always remains usable. At the checkpoint after the laser: preserve solved access to the laser/bridge or place a physical boundary so the current shuttle room is independently solvable. Death there restarts only the shuttle room, never traps the rat behind a newly active laser. Reset Puzzle must state which room is reset.

## Acceptance
Solve with one rat, backtrack through both pairs, move the crate off/on the plate, reset before and after latching, die before/after upper checkpoint, miss shuttle and retry. No three-dimensional collision drift or stale moving-platform carry after a transfer. Verify Next to Level 3 and progress relaunch.
