# Level 1 — Transfer Training

Status: proposed blockout; target 3–5 minutes for a first clear.
Purpose: teach movement, crate weight and one fixed portal pair without timing pressure. Laboratory induction chamber with clear sightlines. One pair A links a lower puzzle deck to an upper exit route; both arrival pads stand on safe floors.

## Rooms and intended solo solution
1. Entry: safe walk/jump practice, three rats visible, basic prompts; falling in the first real pit demonstrates recovery from the nearby checkpoint.
2. Gate demonstration: on a continuous lower deck push the constrained crate onto the plate. A visible connection shows upper laser A turning off. The crate remains on the plate when the rat leaves.
3. Transfer: reach portal A without moving the crate; E sends the rat to the upper deck before the disabled laser. Walk through to the exit. The return portal remains accessible for correction.

## Solvability/reset rules
No switch needs the rat's weight; no elemental immunity; no timed race. The crate track permits moving on/off the plate without losing it. A remains enabled independently of the plate so the player cannot strand themselves by moving the crate. Place checkpoint before the crate lesson; death/reset restores crate off plate, laser active and both pads available. No checkpoint beyond the gate until crossing is secure.

## Required feedback and acceptance
Show crate-centred plate state, linked laser state, paired A labels, E prompt and a clear exit. Player can infer “crate holds laser off while I transfer” without external explanation. Verify solve, return, deliberate unsolve, death/reset from both sides, three-life failure, replay and Next to Level 2. Optional short hint after repeated failed interactions, never auto-solve.
